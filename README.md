# Murphmon

[MurphLeague 2026](https://github.com/BigMurph619/Murphleague-2026) Tourney #4 is Emerald Murphmon - Summer Edition. Base rules and information found [here](https://github.com/TakeJoshyy/TheMurphVerse/tree/main/1.GameModes/Murphmon). 

Murphmon is a randomized Pokemon challenge inspired by Super Kaizo IronMON. It aims to be a bit easier and faster while adding more decision making opportunities to the game. Murphmon can be played 10 different ways, all of which remain officially unbeaten in their base rulesets. Many rules and settings are mindfully adapted for tournament play and the game is intended to be challenging but beatable. Base rules found [here](https://github.com/TakeJoshyy/TheMurphVerse/tree/main/1.GameModes/Murphmon)

"Summer Edition" will feature a pool of 7 Summer Themed 'Tune-ups'. Competitors will be given 3 'Tuning Tokens' per seed that they may spend on Tune-Ups. 

This Season's Tune-Ups are:
|         Tune-Ups          | Details |
|-|-|
| Harvester | May grab 1 tree of berries from each soil patch en route to safari. Pre-pivot only. |           
| Hydration  | May use drink heals outside of battle until Safari. |
| Vacation | Re-rando Counts as 3 tune-ups early game. If used at Safari, must have 6 pivots locked in...no more catches. |
| Band Camp   | Unlock all flutes and bells for mons with a quad weakness.  |
| Gone Surfing   | May use Surf with Safari pivot that is under 500 BST and not water type |
| Farmer's Market  |  You may purchase vitamins from the Slateport market, all one transaction before going north. |
| No Hoenn Around | Forego early pivot options to gain 3 more wild exp kills. No favorites. Commit point first youngster. |

# Tourney Details

T4 is hosted by [roflmaogamers1](https://www.twitch.tv/roflmaogamers1). Dates: July 2nd 11:59 PM EST - July 19th 11:59 PM EST. Sign up deadline is July 10th with details on tourney sign up available in the MurphVerse Discord.

> You must be in the [MurphVerse discord](https://discord.gg/fNX4geCU) and all seeds must be live for the entire run and have a VOD available. Murphleague tourneys are free to enter and open to all entrants unless previously banned.

Competitors will be given 100 seeds. Your score is determined by your 3 highest scoring runs combined.

## Awards

Upon entering this tournament you will claim which charity you are playing for. BigMurph619 will be providing donation to each of the top 3 finishers. Additionally, any donations from the "Pledge Page" in Murph's discord will be added to the prize pool.

Competitors will also earn "League Points" towards the MurphLeague according to their respective placement. League Points determine eligibility and seeding for the Murphleague playoffs which has a larger prize pool including charity donations, an engraved trophy, and more.
